====================================================================================================
 INITIAL FABLE v5 — PILOT RECORDING SHEET  (200 episodes, 40 runs x 5, 8 sessions of 25)
 Purpose: DEPTH-FIRST pilot — 2 slot destinations + BIN, ~65-70 eps EACH: enough data per
 destination to truly TEST color grounding, bin drops, and prompt-following before Phase B.
 before the full Phase-B recording. Generated with seed=42 (reproducible).
====================================================================================================

PHYSICAL LAYOUT (fixed for the WHOLE pilot — do not move anything between sessions)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The two racks sit SIDE BY SIDE in front of the arm, PERPENDICULAR to it, with a GAP
  between them. The ARM is centered BEHIND that middle gap.
- LEFT rack = the rack on the arm's left; RIGHT rack = on its right. Slots 1..6 numbered
  the same way as v4 (keep your existing convention; mark slot 1 with tape if unsure).
- The BIN sits at the SIDE (pick left or right once, mark its floor position with tape,
  never move it during the pilot).
- Cameras: SAME pose as v4 (run the alignment check vs reference frames first).
  Racks: mark their footprint with tape too — they must not drift between sessions.

COLORS (4 chosen for maximum visual separation)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  USE:   RED, CYAN, DARK-GREEN, LIGHT-PURPLE
  DO NOT USE: light pink (confusable with red), yellow (swapped out per Sari 2026-07-05).
  The starred vial (e.g. 4:YELLOW*) is the TARGET the prompt names. Distractor vials are
  NEVER the same color as the target (no ambiguity), duplicates among distractors are fine.

RULES (every episode)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Set the scene EXACTLY as the episode line says: LEFT[slot:color,...] RIGHT[slot:color,...].
 2. HOME the arm before every episode (this is policy — initial-state robustness).
 3. Teleop: grasp the STARRED vial -> place it at the stated destination (slot or bin).
    Slot placements: full insertion. Bin: release above the bin opening.
 4. -> saves the episode, <- redoes it. Redo any sloppy grasp/placement — quality > speed.
 5. Between EPISODES within a run only the vials move (scene reset per the sheet).
 6. Between RUNS the label changes -> new CLI invocation with --resume=true.
 7. After each SESSION (5 runs = 25 eps): run
       .venv/bin/python ee/audit_labels.py --root <dataset> --group
    on the WS to catch label mistakes SAME DAY.
 8. Sources use anchor slots 1,3,4,6 only (2 and 5 are held out on purpose — the
    policy interpolates them; we verify that at eval).

HOW TO READ AN EPISODE LINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ep 003 | L: P* G  C  .  .  R  | R: .  .  .  .  .  .  | -> BIN
  Six columns per rack = slots 1..6.   . = empty slot.
  Letters: R=RED  C=CYAN  G=DARK-GREEN  P=LIGHT-PURPLE.
  The * marks the TARGET vial (the one the run's prompt names) — grasp that one.
  '-> BIN' / '-> LEFT pos N' / '-> RIGHT pos N' = where to place it.
  Episode numbers are 0-indexed and match lerobot's episode_index (audit_labels.py).
