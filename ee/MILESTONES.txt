--------------------------------------------------------------------
 MILESTONE 1 — SETUP
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Our vial-sorting rig is an SO-101 6-DOF arm on an NVIDIA Jetson Thor,
facing two 6-slot racks arranged DIAGONALLY to the arm, with our BIN
for trash beside them on the LEFT. Three cameras record at 640x480 and
30 fps: an overhead top camera, a wrist camera, and a front-facing
Intel RealSense side camera. The task: on a language command, pick the
named colored vial and place it into a specified rack slot; vials that
do not belong go in the bin.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
 MILESTONE 2 — DATA COLLECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Demonstrations are collected by teleoperation. A human drives a leader
arm, the follower mirrors it, and all cameras, joint states, and the
language instruction are recorded into a LeRobot dataset. Here one
episode is replayed with every camera view, the joint trajectory, and
the prompt synced on a single timeline, exactly the format the policy
trains on. Each episode is one atomic skill, put the color vial in the
destination, staged with distractor vials so the policy follows the
prompt rather than memorizing a fixed scene.

FILE:  data_collection_annotated.mp4
FROM:  generated by the visualizer on Thor, viewed in your laptop browser.
HOW:   on Thor run
         lerobot-dataset-viz --repo-id sari-abdan/vial-sort-v5-pilot \
           --root /home/robot/my_local_data_v5 --episode-index 0 \
           --mode distant --web-port 9090
       then open  http://192.168.123.198:9090  on your laptop, choose a
       clean episode with --episode-index N, and screen-record it.
       Save as data_collection_annotated.mp4


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 MILESTONE 3 — DEPTH PERCEPTION (evaluated)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
In the comparison, Depth Anything V2 clearly captures the tubes better
than the Intel RealSense sensor, which sees through the transparent
glass. We train pi0.5 on RGB, since VLA models work primarily on RGB,
and integrate Depth Anything in the next training round.

FILE:  triptych_rgb_da_intel_v5.mp4   (RGB | Depth Anything V2 | Intel RealSense)
FROM:  your Downloads. Also depth_v5.mp4 (RealSense only) and
       depth_anything_v5.mp4 if you want them separately.
HOW:   already on your laptop, trim to a clean ~10 s. This one clip IS
       the milestone: it shows the evaluation and why RGB won.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 MILESTONE 4 — AUTONOMOUS INFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The robot runs the task on its own from a trained policy. We show three,
in the order we built them:
  - ACT   — our baseline. It learned the pick-and-place from the demos and
            executes it autonomously.
  - pi0   — our second policy. It worked well, but it did not follow the
            language prompt.
  - pi0.5 — what we use now: same task, much better at prompt-following, so
            the command actually controls where the vial goes.
Early tests on the v4 dataset, ahead of the v5 retrain.

FILE:  three short clips, ~8-12 s each, one clean run —
         ACT   : the clip you found
         pi0   : the clip from your drive
         pi0.5 : place_good_v3.mp4  (~/Downloads/exec_playable/, H.264)
HOW:   lead with pi0.5 as the current model. If a clip won't open, it is the
       mpeg4 issue — transcode to H.264 (ask and I'll do it).
