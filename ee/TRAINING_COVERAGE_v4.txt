 GENERALIZATION MAP
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------
 TRAINED PROMPTS (should follow):
   - "position 1 of the left rack"
   - "position 3 of the left rack"
   - "position 6 of the left rack"
   - "position 6 of the right rack"
 SOURCE (vial start): both racks, all slots -> grasp generalizes to a vial anywhere.
 NOT TRAINED:
   - right pos 1-5  -> UNSEEN (right rack only ever placed at pos 6). Will fail/guess.
   - left pos 2,4,5 -> never trained, but BETWEEN anchors 1/3/6 -> may interpolate; verify.

 TO EXTEND LATER — the GENERALIZATION PLAN:
   GOAL: the PROMPT must be able to name EVERY destination position, so record ALL 6
   destination positions in each rack. The SOURCE is what we keep cheap (anchors only)
   and let the policy interpolate — exactly how v1 fullft pi0 (100 eps) generalized:
   trained SOURCE 1/3/4/5, never saw source 2, and handled it correctly on the robot.

   THE PLAN:
   - SOURCE (vial start): ONLY anchors 1, 3, 4, 6 (vary every episode). The policy
     interpolates the unseen source slots 2 and 5 — no need to record them.
   - DESTINATION: record EVERY position 1,2,3,4,5,6 on the OTHER rack, BOTH directions,
     so a prompt for ANY slot is in the training data (destinations are NOT interpolated):
         left  pos 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
         right pos 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
   - Already have: left 1/3/6 + right 6. GAP to record = left 2,4,5 + right 1,2,3,4,5.
   - Each destination = one session, source cycling 1,3,4,6 within it. ~30 demos/dest
     (SO-101+pi0 curve: ~100 eps/task -> ~70%). 12 destinations -> big multi-session record.
   - Keep paraphrasing. After: convert joint->EE, paraphrase the NEW set, merge into the
     v4 dataset, retrain pi05 (train_pi05_v4.sh), eval by real-robot prompt-following.

   WHY: every DESTINATION is explicitly trained (so the prompt controls all 6 slots),
   while the SOURCE generalizes from anchors 1/3/4/6 (the v1-proven interpolation) so you
   don't have to record sources 2 and 5.
