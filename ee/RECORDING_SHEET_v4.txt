each (we already have ~18-20 each in v4-ee). SO-101+pi0 curve: ~100 eps/task -> ~70%.

BLUE VIAL = DISTRACTOR for generalization. Keep a blue vial somewhere in the
workspace and MOVE IT every couple of episodes (cues below) so the model learns
to ignore clutter and only act on the RED vial named in the prompt. Never put the
blue vial in the exact SOURCE or DEST slot the red vial uses that episode.

KEY RULE: DEST is fixed per session (what the prompt commands). SOURCE varies each
episode (cycle 1 -> 3 -> 4 -> 6). This is what makes the grab generalize.

Camera: drift inside the +/-5% aug band — record at the SAME pose as existing data.
Do NOT bump the camera between sessions.
